Connect Firebase to turn on the dashboard

The dashboard stores projects, team members and orders in Firebase. Until it’s connected, the site shows the built-in content from js/content.js.

  1. Create a free project at console.firebase.google.com and add a Web app.
  2. Paste its config into js/config.js.
  3. Turn on Authentication → Email/Password and add your admin user.
  4. Create a Firestore database and paste in the rules from firestore.rules, with your admin email.

Full steps are in README.md.
